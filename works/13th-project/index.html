Making the open-discover experience effective more guided, relevant, and engaging.

Project background

The challenge

Product issue
Map discovery gave users freedom but lacked a clear starting point, leading to low engagement.
Product goal
Increase profile views and engagement by helping users find relevant people more easily.
Design challenge
How might we guide users toward meaningful connections without taking away the freedom to explore?
Key Stage #1

Exploring: Finding direction in an open map

Explore why an open map can feel aimless, and how users might benefit from clearer starting points and contextual cues.

  • Too many or too little people around you
  • No information other than face (sometimes not even a face)
  • Probabely the same people you saw yesterday
Option 1. Continuse browsing feed
  • Curated feed with proximity order
  • Top-level intriging information with actionable buttons
Option 2. Focused browsable carousel
  • Curated profiles in proximity order
  • Profile highlight with actionable buttons above the fold
Option 3. 3D Zoomed-in navigation
  • Zoom into a profile once at a time
  • Profile highlight HUD concept

Re-targeting: Turning the goal-oriented UX

Go deep to interact or go wide to find more - one thing at a time.

Key Stage #4

Final design: Focused mode with exit

Learnings

"Have options, not distractions"

More options without mindful guadience, are distraction. The key is to give users enough direction to take the next step, while keeping them in control of where they go.

Good discovery isn't about showing users everything. It's about helping them find what matters to them.
